# What are LAN, MAN, and WAN ?

LAN, MAN, and WAN are different types of computer networks. They are mainly classified according to the geographical area they cover.

1. LAN — Local Area Network

LAN stands for Local Area Network.

A LAN connects computers and other devices within a small geographical area.

Examples

Home

School

Office

Computer laboratory

Small building

Example
Computer ──┐
Laptop ────┤
Printer ───┼── Switch / Router
Phone ─────┤
Server ────┘

Features

Covers a small area

Usually provides high speed

Usually privately owned

Relatively inexpensive

Easy to manage

2. MAN — Metropolitan Area Network

MAN stands for Metropolitan Area Network.

A MAN connects multiple LANs across a city or metropolitan area.

Examples

University campuses across a city

City-wide organizational networks

Networks connecting different offices within a city

Example
LAN ───────┐
           │
LAN ── MAN Network ── LAN
           │
LAN ───────┘

Features

Larger than a LAN

Usually covers a city or metropolitan area

Connects multiple LANs

More complex than a LAN

Can be operated by an organization or network provider

3. WAN — Wide Area Network

WAN stands for Wide Area Network.

A WAN connects networks across a large geographical area, such as different cities, countries, or continents.

Examples

Company networks connecting offices in different cities

International corporate networks

The Internet

Example
Office A ──┐
           │
Office B ──┼── WAN ── Office C
           │
Office D ──┘

Features

Covers a very large geographical area

Can connect different cities and countries

More complex than LAN and MAN

Can use telecommunications infrastructure

Usually more expensive to operate

LAN vs MAN vs WAN
Feature	LAN	MAN	WAN
Full Form	Local Area Network	Metropolitan Area Network	Wide Area Network
Coverage	Small area	City / metropolitan area	Large geographical area
Example	Home or office	City-wide network	Internet
Speed	Generally high	Generally high	Varies
Cost	Low	Medium	High
Connects	Devices and computers	Multiple LANs	Multiple LANs/MANs
Area	Building	City	Country / World
Easy Way to Remember
LAN → Building / Home
MAN → City
WAN → Country / World

In Short

LAN = Network covering a small area

MAN = Network covering a city

WAN = Network covering a large geographical area

LAN < MAN < WAN in terms of geographical coverage.
