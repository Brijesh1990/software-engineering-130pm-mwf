# What Is Google Business Profile?

The service people sometimes call “Google Manager” for business listings is **Google Business Profile**. It was formerly known as **Google My Business**. It lets eligible businesses manage how their information appears on Google Search and Google Maps.

A business profile can show details such as the business name, address or service area, hours, phone number, website, photos, services, and customer reviews. Creating or managing a profile is generally free, although optional advertising and other paid services are separate.

## Who Can Create a Business Profile?

Google Business Profile is intended for businesses that interact with customers in person. This includes:

- Businesses customers visit at a staffed location during stated hours
- Service-area businesses that travel to customers, such as a plumber or home-cleaning service
- Businesses that have both a customer-facing location and a service area

Online-only businesses that do not meet customers in person generally are not eligible. A virtual office or mailbox alone does not make a business eligible as a staffed storefront. Check Google's current eligibility and representation rules before creating a profile, since requirements can change.

## How to List a Business on Google

### 1. Sign in with a Business Account

Use a Google Account that the owner or authorized representative can continue to access. Keep account credentials private and give staff access through profile permissions rather than sharing a password.

### 2. Find or Add the Business

Open the official Google Business Profile website at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com/) or follow Google's business-profile prompts in Search or Maps. Search for the business first:

- If a profile already exists, request access or ownership instead of creating a duplicate.
- If no profile exists, choose the option to add or create the business.

### 3. Enter Accurate Business Details

Provide the real-world business name and choose the most accurate primary category. Add other relevant categories only when they genuinely describe the business.

Depending on the business model, enter:

- A customer-facing address, if customers are served there
- A service area, if the business travels to customers
- Phone number and website
- Business hours and other requested details

Follow Google's address and service-area rules. A service-area business that does not receive customers at its address should generally hide that address from public display. Do not use a false address, keyword-stuffed business name, or an address where the business is not eligible to operate.

### 4. Verify the Business

Google requires verification to confirm that the business is genuine and that the person managing the listing is authorized. Google determines which verification methods are available for each business; options may include video, phone, email, or mail. Not every method is available to every business.

Follow the instructions shown in the profile. Do not create another profile to get around a verification delay or problem.

### 5. Complete and Maintain the Profile

After verification, review the profile and add useful, accurate information, such as:

- Regular and special hours
- Services or products, where supported
- Clear photos that represent the real business
- A concise description of what the business offers
- An appointment, ordering, or other relevant link, if available

Keep the details current. Respond professionally to reviews, and do not post fake reviews or offer improper incentives for reviews. Avoid including confidential customer information in public replies.

## Tips for a Reliable Listing

- Use consistent, truthful business details across the web.
- Choose the most specific accurate business category.
- Create only one profile per eligible business location, unless Google's rules allow otherwise.
- Give access only to trusted owners or managers, and remove access when it is no longer needed.
- Watch for verification requests and profile changes in the account.
- Be cautious of people who claim they can guarantee a particular ranking or demand payment to create a standard profile.

## What a Google Business Profile Does Not Guarantee

A profile can help customers find business information in Google Search and Maps, but it does not guarantee a specific search position, number of customers, or positive reviews. Local visibility can depend on factors such as relevance, distance, prominence, competition, and the accuracy and completeness of information.

## Conclusion

Google Business Profile is Google's service for managing eligible businesses' public information in Search and Maps. To list a business, sign in, check for an existing profile, add accurate details, complete Google's verification process, and keep the listing up to date. Always follow the current Google guidelines for eligibility and business representation.
